Privacy Policy & POPI Act Notice

Effective Date: January 2026

1. Introduction

Shirley Pre-School ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our parents, donors, and raffle participants. This policy explains how we handle your personal information in accordance with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only when you voluntarily provide it to us via our contact forms or raffle entries. This may include:

  • Full Names and Contact Numbers.
  • Email Addresses.
  • Proof of Payment (for Raffle entries).

3. Purpose of Processing

Your data is used strictly for the following:

  • Responding to enrolment enquiries.
  • Managing and verifying "Digital Launchpad" Raffle entries.
  • Updating you on school-related news (only if consented).

4. Data Security & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We use secure third-party services like Formspree to process form submissions. While we take all reasonable steps to secure your data, no digital transmission is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal info we hold about you.
  • Request the correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to the processing of your information.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the details provided on our Contact page.
