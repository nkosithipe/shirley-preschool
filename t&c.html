Raffle Terms & Conditions

Project: Shirley Pre-School "Digital Launchpad" Fundraiser 2026

1. The Promoter

This raffle is promoted by Shirley Pre-School (NPO), Shirley Village, Elim, for the purpose of school facility upgrades and educational resources.

2. Entry & Eligibility

  • Tickets are priced at R100.00 per entry.
  • Participants must be 18 years or older and South African residents.
  • The raffle follows a "Winner-Takes-All" format. No second or third prizes will be awarded.
  • The draw will take place on 19 April 2026, or once the target of R 35,000.00 entries is met, whichever occurs first.
  • The Promoter reserves the right to extend the draw date by no more than 30 days should the minimum fundraising threshold not be met.

3. The Prize (The "Digital Launchpad")

The winner will receive a comprehensive digital presence build-out, including:

  • One (1) Mobile-first, responsive business website design.
  • Professional Copywriting and SEO (Search Engine Optimization) application.
  • Google Ecosystem Setup; Business Profile & Analytics.
  • Design of either one (1) Online Ad Banner or one (1) Promotional Video (not both).
  • Three (3) months of free website maintenance and platform operation.

4. Winner's Obligations & Exclusions

To fulfill the prize, the winner is responsible for the following costs:

  • Hosting & Domain: Registration and monthly hosting fees for the website.
  • Content Production: High-quality images must be provided. If the promoter must travel to take photographs, the winner must cover all transport and logistics costs.
  • Third-Party Ad Spend: Any costs for Google Ads, YouTube bidding, or Social Media ad campaigns.
  • Analytics: Any premium Google Analytics or keyword bidding costs.

5. Selection of the Winner

The winner will be selected via random draw. To successfully claim the prize, the selected entrant must correctly answer a qualifying question regarding Shirley Pre-School's mission or history.

6. Fulfilling the Prize

The build-out phase will commence within 30 days of the draw. The 3-month maintenance period begins immediately upon the website "Go-Live" date. Any work requested beyond the initial scope will be subject to a separate service agreement.
